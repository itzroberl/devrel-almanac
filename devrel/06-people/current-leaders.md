## Caveats

People in DevRel move companies often. Treat anything in this list as a snapshot. Some specific patterns to watch:

- Senior DevRel leaders frequently rotate between AI companies (OpenAI → Google → Anthropic and vice versa) and large platform companies (Microsoft, GitHub, AWS).
- Many of the people in this list moved into AI-specific DevRel between 2022 and 2024 from broader-platform roles.
- Foundation Steering Committee membership is a one-to-two-year role; rotations happen.
