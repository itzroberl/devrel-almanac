# Language and Framework Conferences

For most major programming languages and significant frameworks, an established annual conference (or family of regional conferences) serves as the gathering point for the community. These are critical venues for DevRel teams whose product is adopted by communities organised around specific languages or frameworks.

## Why these conferences matter for DevRel

For any company whose product touches a particular language or framework, the language's flagship conference is one of the most leveraged DevRel investments available. A sponsored speaking slot at GopherCon, KotlinConf, or PyCon US reaches a deeply qualified audience that paid marketing channels cannot replicate.

Practitioners generally rank these conferences higher than vendor flagships for **community trust** even though vendor flagships rank higher for **scale and product news**.
