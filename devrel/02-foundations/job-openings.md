# Current DevRel Job Market (Snapshot, Mid-2026)

A market-condition snapshot of where Developer Relations, Developer Advocacy, and Developer Experience roles are open. Treat this as a snapshot: live postings change daily. This file gives the structure of the market, where to find listings, and indicative salary and seniority bands. Pair it with a live job-board search for current openings.

## How to find live listings

These are the actively-maintained DevRel-specific listings and aggregators in 2026:

- **DevRel.jobs** — Curated DevRel-specific board.
- **devrelcareers.com** — Job board and career resources.
- **DevRel Collective Slack** — `#jobs` channel; many roles posted internally before going public.
- **Hoopy / DeveloperRelations.com** — Periodic job postings via the DevRelCon mailing list.
- **The Developer Relations Foundation** (under Linux Foundation) — Job board forming as of 2025–2026.
- **Hacker News "Who's Hiring?"** — Monthly thread; substantial DevRel postings.
- **LinkedIn search** by title: "Developer Advocate," "Developer Relations," "Developer Experience," "Developer Evangelist," "Community Manager (developer)," "Developer Educator," "Technical Writer (developer)."
- **Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)** — Strong for startup DevRel roles.
- **Built In, Built In NYC/SF/LA/Chicago** — Regional tech job boards.
- **RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Remote Rocketship** — Remote-first DevRel postings.
- **Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby company career pages** — Direct postings from companies.
- **Web3.career** — Active for blockchain/crypto DevRel.

For compensation triangulation: **levels.fyi**, **Glassdoor**, **AmbitionBox** (India), **kununu** (DACH), **Talent.com**, **Otta** (UK / EU).

## Hiring patterns to know

A few observations worth knowing if you are looking:

- **AI companies hire faster than they hire well.** Senior AI-DevRel openings are abundant; many of them are filled by hires who lack the specific AI-engineering depth the role requires. Differentiate on demonstrated AI-engineering portfolio.
- **Remote-first companies have specific cultural fit demands.** Async writing skill is a real filter at fully-remote companies (GitLab, Vercel, Cloudflare, Postman, HashiCorp). A strong written-portfolio applicant has meaningful advantage.
- **Marketing-reporting roles offer lower comp than engineering-reporting roles at the same level.** Check the reporting line during the recruiter call.
- **Director / VP roles often go through founder networks first.** Public postings reflect about 60–70% of senior DevRel openings; the rest are filled through referrals.
- **AI-Engineer-specific roles increasingly require a portfolio of LLM-application work.** Generic developer-content portfolios underperform.
- **Remote-from-LATAM into US-HQ-roles** has become a notable hiring lane in 2024–2026, especially at AI companies and PLG developer products. Strong English written portfolio matters more than location.

## Roles in adjacent functions worth considering

For DevRel professionals who feel limited by current DevRel postings, several adjacent functions have been expanding hiring substantially since 2023:

- **Developer Experience Engineer** at product / engineering orgs (often pays more than equivalent DevRel level).
- **Solutions Engineer / Pre-sales Engineer** at developer-product companies (higher OTE on commission structures).
- **Product Manager, Developer Platform** — Many former DevRel professionals transition here.
- **Technical Writer / Documentation Engineer** — Mature roles at large developer companies (Stripe, Twilio, AWS).
- **Customer Engineer** (Google Cloud's term; equivalent at other clouds) — Pre-sales technical role.
- **Open Source Program Manager (OSPM)** — In OSPOs at companies that fund or contribute to open source.
- **AI Engineer / ML Engineer** — Senior DevRel professionals with strong AI-application portfolios are increasingly moving to direct AI-engineering roles.
