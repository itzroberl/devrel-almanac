# Developer Relations at DevTools and Developer-Platform Companies

The companies in this section operate developer products whose users *are* developers. Unlike cloud providers (who also serve devs but are full-stack businesses) or API companies (who serve a single integration point), these companies build platforms, IDEs, frameworks, and developer infrastructure as their core business. Their DevRel functions are correspondingly central.

---

## GitHub (Microsoft, since 2018)

GitHub's developer relations function is large, structurally important, and historically influential.

- **DevRel leadership.** Cassidy Williams serves as Senior Director of Developer Advocacy in the mid-2020s, with senior advocates including Christina Warren, Burke Holland (cross-Microsoft), and many other named voices.
- **GitHub Stars.** Recognition program for the most influential GitHub-using developers globally. Provides amplification, early access, and direct engineering input.
- **GitHub Campus Experts.** Student leader program. Requires post-secondary student status, more than one year remaining before graduation, six months of GitHub use, and an application with video resume. Annual July application window.
- **GitHub Universe.** Annual flagship conference (San Francisco, late October). The 2026 edition was scheduled for October 28–29.
- **GitHub Satellite.** Regional event format launched 2017; satellite editions in India, Berlin, Tokyo, etc.
- **GitHub Octoverse.** Annual state-of-software-development report.
- **GitHub Education.** Free Pro accounts for students, Student Developer Pack with dozens of partner-product benefits.
- **GitHub Copilot DevRel.** Significant team dedicated specifically to Copilot's adoption.
- **Brian Douglas (bdougie).** The first Developer Advocate at GitHub (2018–2022), later founded OpenSauced. One of the most influential alumni of GitHub's DevRel function.

---

## GitLab

GitLab operates open-core development. Its DevRel function is significant but smaller than GitHub's.

- **GitLab Heroes.** Recognition program.
- **GitLab Connect.** Customer events.
- **Unfiltered open development.** GitLab's company handbook (handbook.gitlab.com) is the canonical example of public-by-default operations; the company's openness functions as continuous DevRel.

---

## JetBrains

JetBrains (founded 2000) operates one of the longest-running IDE-vendor DevRel functions.

- **JetBrains Academy.** Training platform.
- **Campus Ambassadors.** University-focused student program.
- **Content Creators Program.** 250+ creators producing JetBrains-tool tutorials and reviews.
- **KotlinConf.** Annual conference for the Kotlin programming language (which JetBrains created). 2026 edition in Munich.
- **JetBrains AI tooling.** JetBrains AI Assistant, Junie agentic coding tool — increasingly central to DevRel emphasis.
- **Arun Gupta.** VP of Developer Experience at JetBrains, joined from prior roles at Intel, Apple, Amazon, and Red Hat. Steering Committee member of the Developer Relations Foundation.

---

## Vercel and the Next.js ecosystem

Vercel (founded 2015) operates a small, high-leverage DevRel function organised around the Next.js framework and the Vercel deployment platform.

- **DevRel leadership.** Lee Robinson (Head of DevRel, also VP Product). One of the most-followed individual voices in JavaScript / React.
- **Advocates.** Delba de Oliveira (Senior Developer Advocate), Steven Tey (formerly Developer Advocate, also founder of Dub.co).
- **Next.js Conf.** Annual virtual / hybrid conference for the Next.js community.
- **Vercel Ship.** Annual customer / partner event.
- **AI SDK.** Vercel AI SDK has become a primary surface for client-side AI integration in JavaScript, with substantial associated DevRel content.

---

## Netlify

Netlify (founded 2014) helped popularise the Jamstack movement and operates a respected DevRel function.

- **Jamstack Conf.** Annual conference (multiple editions: San Francisco, London, virtual).
- **Netlify Developers (DevRel team).** Long-running, with public-facing advocates like Sarah Drasner (former VP of Developer Experience, now at Google) and Cassidy Williams (now at GitHub) shaping the function in the 2018–2022 era.
- **Netlify Build.** Customer / community events.

---

## HashiCorp (IBM, since 2025)

HashiCorp (founded 2012 by Mitchell Hashimoto and Armon Dadgar) operates the largest infrastructure-tooling DevRel function. Acquired by IBM in 2025.

- **DevRel structure.** ~50 staff contributing to community programs across the company, approximately 35% of total marketing headcount.
- **HashiCorp Ambassadors.** Program launched 2020; 102 ambassadors across 31 countries by 2024.
- **HashiCorp User Groups (HUGs).** 173 groups across 61 countries, reaching 49,000+ people.
- **HashiConf.** Annual flagship conference; co-located with IBM TechXchange post-acquisition (2026 in Atlanta, October 26–29).
- **HashiCorp Learn (learn.hashicorp.com / developer.hashicorp.com).** Comprehensive tutorial library across Terraform, Vault, Consul, Nomad, Packer, Boundary, Waypoint, Vagrant.
- **Notable early advocates.** Nic Jackson, Anubhav Mishra — whose technical demos and tutorials in the mid-2010s shaped the function.

- **DASH conference.** Annual flagship event (NYC).
- **Datadog Learning Center.** Self-paced training.

- **New Relic Champions** program.

## Snyk

- **SnykCon.** Annual conference.
- **DeveloperRelations.com (Snyk).** Strong content focus on developer security.

## Other notable developer-platform DevRel programs

- **Cloudflare.** Covered in [`./cloud-providers.md`](cloud-providers.md).
- **Hashicorp/IBM** above.
- **Cloudinary, Fastly, Akamai.** CDN / edge platforms with established DevRel.
- **Linear, Notion, Airtable.** Productivity-platform-with-developer-API model; lighter-weight DevRel.
- **Sentry.** Error monitoring; *SentryConf* annual; large open-source community.
- **PagerDuty.** Incident response; *PagerDuty Summit*.
- **LaunchDarkly.** Feature flags; *Galaxy* annual conference, *Trajectory* developer event.
- **Split, Flagsmith, ConfigCat, GrowthBook.** Feature-flag market peers with smaller DevRel.
- **Replit.** Browser-based coding environment; long-running DevRel via Replit Bounties, Replit Apps, and *Replit AI* programs.
- **StackBlitz, CodeSandbox.** In-browser dev environments (see [`../08-tools/code-sandboxes.md`](../08-tools/code-sandboxes.md)).
- **Render, Fly.io, Railway.** Modern PaaS competitors to Heroku; smaller but tightly focused DevRel.
- **Heroku (Salesforce).** Historically one of the most influential DX teams in technology. Substantially reduced through Salesforce restructurings 2024–2026.
- **DigitalOcean.** Covered in [`./cloud-providers.md`](cloud-providers.md).
- **Hugging Face.** Covered in [`./ai-companies.md`](ai-companies.md).
- **Notion, Figma, Linear developer APIs.** Smaller DevRel functions where the API is a secondary product surface.
